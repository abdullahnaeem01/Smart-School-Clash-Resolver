    🏃 P.E Rules

    Grade 4 and above: boys' and girls' classes never share the ground. Grades 1–3 (boys and girls together is fine) share the ground only with other Grade 1–3 classes, never with senior sections.

    🔀 Parallel Subjects (same period)

    Subjects listed together are taught in the same period to different students of one class (e.g. Biology + ICT). They count once toward the 35 periods, and both teachers are scheduled in that slot.
